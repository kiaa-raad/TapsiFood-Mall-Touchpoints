Deliveroo

The Spirit Show

Projects

After three previous projects, It was time for us to launch with Deliveroo their Alcohol delivery service arm running parallel with their unrivaled food service.

Working alongside Deliveroo we helped develop a series of interactive game areas to engage the attendees and bring a ‘light-hearted’ fun into the scene. These games included a custom designed giant buzz wires built on colourful plinths. In addition we built a series of tube like structures for ‘Roo Pong’; a Deliveroo inspired game of cornhole with branded bean bags. These games along with Deliveroo’s eye-catching branding created a playful, open and inviting space, which attracted many visitors throughout the event.

We loved working with the team at Deliveroo, and here’s what they had to say about us:

“The London Display Company have been an absolute pleasure to work with, and continue to be consistently reliable and very helpful”.
