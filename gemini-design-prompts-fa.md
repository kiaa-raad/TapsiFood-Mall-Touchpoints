# پرامپت‌های Gemini | پروپوزال تپسی و تپسی‌فود در مال

گایدلاین رسمی، لوگوی اصلی و مرجع‌های منتخب همان شات را پیوست کنید. ابتدا پرامپت مادر، سپس هر شات را جدا ارسال کنید. تصویر منتخب قبلی را برای ثبات طراحی پیوست کنید. رندرهای قبلیِ داخلی فقط برای تشخیص ایرادها هستند و نباید عیناً بازتولید شوند.

## پرامپت مادر

You are a senior environmental graphic designer, retail experience designer, and industrial designer. Design a coherent family of physical touchpoints for Tapsi and TapsiFood in a large Iranian shopping mall.

INPUTS I WILL ATTACH:
1. The official brand design guidelines (PDF).
2. Original approved logo files, preferably SVG or a transparent PNG.
3. Selected benchmark photographs, each identified by brand and location.
4. If available, site photographs, a floor plan, and known dimensions.

FIRST: Read the brand guidelines and identify the approved colors with exact values, logo variants, minimum clear space, typography, graphic language, and prohibited treatments. Explicit rules in the official guidelines override colors or style mentioned in this prompt or seen in benchmarks. If the guidelines do not specify an item, state your design assumption. Do not invent an official brand rule. If any attached file is unreadable, identify it instead of claiming to have read it.

USE OF REFERENCES:
Benchmark images illustrate principles, not artwork to copy. Do not reproduce competitor logos, proprietary graphics, distinctive mascots, or their exact structures. Translate principles such as visual hierarchy, consistent location codes, a complete wayfinding system, practical counters and integrated seating into an original Tapsi/TapsiFood design.

SHARED DESIGN DIRECTION:
Create one recognizable family across passenger stations, a staffed food dispatch hub, and an engagement counter. Use consistent frame geometry, sign proportions, corner treatment and typography, with function-specific layouts. Distinguish Tapsi mobility and TapsiFood using the exact identities and relationship defined by the attached guidelines. If only one logo is provided, do not fabricate the other wordmark.
Use the approved brand orange as a primary accent when permitted by the guidelines, balanced with neutral surfaces. Do not use green as a generic brand color. Make the result buildable and suitable for a busy mall, rather than a futuristic sculpture.

LOGO AND TEXT:
Place the supplied original logo as artwork; never retype, redraw, distort, outline or reinterpret it. Preserve aspect ratio and approved clear space. Use the approved Persian font, or Vazirmatn only when the guidelines do not specify one. Render Persian right-to-left, with correct letter joining. If exact text/logo fidelity cannot be achieved, leave a clean reserved area and state that original artwork must be applied in postproduction; do not output corrupted lettering as final signage.
Illustrative location code: T1. It is a design placeholder, not a real mall address. Repeat it consistently in the relevant mockups. Short exact Persian messages may be specified by the shot prompt.

REALISM:
Show credible human scale, usable openings, plausible construction joints and service access. Use people only to explain the activity and scale; do not obscure key design details. Show clear pedestrian circulation and functional waiting positions. Image output is a concept visualization, not a verified construction or electrical drawing. Do not invent costs, certified dimensions, or measured performance benefits.
Maintain the same selected design across views. Produce each requested view as a separate high-resolution image, ideally 16:9 at 3840 x 2160 if supported; use the nearest supported resolution otherwise. Do not combine views into a collage unless explicitly asked.
Before generation, briefly state the design concept and the specific rules extracted from the attachments. Then generate the requested shot.

IF PREVIOUS INTERNALLY GENERATED RENDERS ARE ATTACHED:
Treat them as rejected diagnostic references, not as the target design or an approved style. Extract only useful context, then redesign.
- The previous outdoor shelter confuses passenger mobility with food branding and burger advertising; it lacks a visible location code, a complete wayfinding system, and clearly designed charging/help amenities. The new passenger station must express its mobility function, using only the approved identity.
- The previous indoor counter looks like a food ordering/sales stall with menu tablets and a burger screen. The replacement must communicate services, campaigns and assisted in-person credit activation. Do not show a restaurant menu or a food-sales queue.
- The previous food kiosk includes an attendant and shelves, which are useful starting points, but the render does not demonstrate runner receiving, order verification, shelf organization or courier waiting. Resolve these operational relationships rather than simply changing the facade.
- AI-generated wordmarks, inconsistent brand marks and dimension labels in those images are not approved inputs. Do not copy their lettering, facade proportions or displayed measurements. Do not add dimension arrows unless measured site information has been supplied.
A design is not complete simply because it has an orange fascia and a logo. Show how someone finds the place and uses the service.
