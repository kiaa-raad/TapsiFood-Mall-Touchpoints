CONCEPT / PROPOSED

«ایستگاه انتخاب» تپسی‌فود

یک کانتر باز که با یک سؤال شروع می‌کند: «امروز چطور کمکت کنیم؟»

پیشنهاد امروز خرید اعتباری راهنمای خدمات

مخاطب از روی نمایشگر یا پنل انتخاب، موضوعش را مشخص می‌کند. راهنما پیشنهاد مرتبط را نشان می‌دهد و کاربر با QR اختصاصی، ادامه مسیر را روی گوشی خودش می‌بیند. برای فعال‌سازی یا توضیح بیشتر، یک موقعیت آرام‌تر در کنار کانتر داریم.

طراحی فضا

  • قاب شاخص و تابلوی قابل دید از چند جهت
  • کانتر باز برای گفت‌وگوی کوتاه
  • نمایشگر یا پنل تعاملی با سه انتخاب روشن
  • موقعیت کناری راهنمایی با تبلت
  • کمد پنهان اقلام و پنل کمپین قابل تعویض
  • خروجی مفید: پیشنهاد، راهنما یا مسیر فعال‌سازی

تجربه مراجعه‌کننده

  1. از فاصله دور متوجه می‌شود اینجا خدمات تپسی‌فود است.
  2. با یک انتخاب ساده وارد گفت‌وگو می‌شود.
  3. پیشنهاد یا سرویس مناسب را می‌شناسد.
  4. در صورت نیاز، راهنمایی فعال‌سازی خرید اعتباری می‌گیرد.
  5. با یک اقدام مشخص روی گوشی خودش ادامه می‌دهد.

ارائه اعتبار و شرایط آن به سرویس فعال و پذیرندگان هر مال وابسته است؛ در این مرحله، جایگاه معرفی و راهنمایی آن طراحی می‌شود.

یک برند لوازم موبایل یا ارائه‌دهنده شارژ می‌تواند ماژول کوچکی به کانتر اضافه کند: کاربر گوشی را شارژ می‌کند، پیشنهاد روز را می‌بیند و در صورت تمایل با راهنما گفت‌وگو می‌کند. هویت میزبان تپسی‌فود ثابت می‌ماند و ماژول شریک قابل نصب و حذف است.

این یک ایده پیشنهادی است، نه همکاری موجود. نام شریک تعیین نشده و اجرای آن شرط راه‌اندازی کانتر نیست. در فاز فعلی، خود کانتر باید برای خدمات، کمپین و معرفی اعتبار کار کند.

GEMINI / IMAGE GENERATION PROMPTS

از پروپوزال به تصویر؛ پرامپت‌های آماده Gemini

گایدلاین برند و لوگوی اصلی را پیوست کن؛ پرامپت مادر را یک بار بده و بعد هر شات را جدا بساز. برای نماهای بعدی، تصویر منتخب قبلی را هم پیوست کن تا زبان طراحی ثابت بماند.

دریافت تمام پرامپت‌ها

  1. ورودی‌ها: گایدلاین رسمی، لوگوی تأییدشده و عکس‌های مرجع مرتبط؛ در صورت وجود عکس و پلان محل.
  2. مرجع برند: ضوابط فایل پیوست بر رنگ و فونت پیشنهادی این صفحه اولویت دارد. رنگ فعلی صفحه از نارنجی لوگو گرفته شده است.
  3. ثبات تصویر: ابتدا یک کانسپت را انتخاب کن؛ بعد نمای نزدیک، نمای داخلی و جزئیات همان طرح را بخواه.
  4. کیفیت نوشتار: لوگو نباید توسط مدل بازطراحی شود. اگر حروف فارسی دقیق نبود، محل نوشته خالی بماند و آرت‌ورک اصلی بعداً قرار گیرد.

You are a senior environmental graphic designer, retail experience designer, and industrial designer. Design a coherent family of physical touchpoints for Tapsi and TapsiFood in a large Iranian shopping mall.

INPUTS I WILL ATTACH:
1. The official brand design guidelines (PDF).
2. Original approved logo files, preferably SVG or a transparent PNG.
3. Selected benchmark photographs, each identified by brand and location.
4. If available, site photographs, a floor plan, and known dimensions.

FIRST: Read the brand guidelines and identify the approved colors with exact values, logo variants, minimum clear space, typography, graphic language, and prohibited treatments. Explicit rules in the official guidelines override colors or style mentioned in this prompt or seen in benchmarks. If the guidelines do not specify an item, state your design assumption. Do not invent an official brand rule. If any attached file is unreadable, identify it instead of claiming to have read it.

USE OF REFERENCES:
Benchmark images illustrate principles, not artwork to copy. Do not reproduce competitor logos, proprietary graphics, distinctive mascots, or their exact structures. Translate principles such as visual hierarchy, consistent location codes, a complete wayfinding system, practical counters and integrated seating into an original Tapsi/TapsiFood design.

SHARED DESIGN DIRECTION:
Create one recognizable family across passenger stations, a staffed food dispatch hub, and an engagement counter. Use consistent frame geometry, sign proportions, corner treatment and typography, with function-specific layouts. Distinguish Tapsi mobility and TapsiFood using the exact identities and relationship defined by the attached guidelines. If only one logo is provided, do not fabricate the other wordmark.
Use the approved brand orange as a primary accent when permitted by the guidelines, balanced with neutral surfaces. Do not use green as a generic brand color. Make the result buildable and suitable for a busy mall, rather than a futuristic sculpture.

LOGO AND TEXT:
Place the supplied original logo as artwork; never retype, redraw, distort, outline or reinterpret it. Preserve aspect ratio and approved clear space. Use the approved Persian font, or Vazirmatn only when the guidelines do not specify one. Render Persian right-to-left, with correct letter joining. If exact text/logo fidelity cannot be achieved, leave a clean reserved area and state that original artwork must be applied in postproduction; do not output corrupted lettering as final signage.
Illustrative location code: T1. It is a design placeholder, not a real mall address. Repeat it consistently in the relevant mockups. Short exact Persian messages may be specified by the shot prompt.

REALISM:
Show credible human scale, usable openings, plausible construction joints and service access. Use people only to explain the activity and scale; do not obscure key design details. Show clear pedestrian circulation and functional waiting positions. Image output is a concept visualization, not a verified construction or electrical drawing. Do not invent costs, certified dimensions, or measured performance benefits.
Maintain the same selected design across views. Produce each requested view as a separate high-resolution image, ideally 16:9 at 3840 x 2160 if supported; use the nearest supported resolution otherwise. Do not combine views into a collage unless explicitly asked.
Before generation, briefly state the design concept and the specific rules extracted from the attachments. Then generate the requested shot.

IF PREVIOUS INTERNALLY GENERATED RENDERS ARE ATTACHED:
Treat them as rejected diagnostic references, not as the target design or an approved style. Extract only useful context, then redesign.
- The previous outdoor shelter confuses passenger mobility with food branding and burger advertising; it lacks a visible location code, a complete wayfinding system, and clearly designed charging/help amenities. The new passenger station must express its mobility function, using only the approved identity.
- The previous indoor counter looks like a food ordering/sales stall with menu tablets and a burger screen. The replacement must communicate services, campaigns and assisted in-person credit activation. Do not show a restaurant menu or a food-sales queue.
- The previous food kiosk includes an attendant and shelves, which are useful starting points, but the render does not demonstrate runner receiving, order verification, shelf organization or courier waiting. Resolve these operational relationships rather than simply changing the facade.
- AI-generated wordmarks, inconsistent brand marks and dimension labels in those images are not approved inputs. Do not copy their lettering, facade proportions or displayed measurements. Do not add dimension arrows unless measured site information has been supplied.
A design is not complete simply because it has an orange fascia and a logo. Show how someone finds the place and uses the service.

Design a TapsiFood staffed food-dispatch container beside the service access of a large mall.
The workflow is fixed: an internal runner brings prepared orders from multiple mall restaurants to the hub; one permanent human attendant receives, identifies and stores them on labeled shelves; delivery couriers collect orders from the attendant at the handoff window. This is not an unattended locker and not a cooking kitchen.
Use a simple prefabricated container with a wide sliding handoff window and a separate staff/service door. Include a dry, sheltered courier waiting strip, a clear arrival sign, and a shelf layout visible through the window. Include an attendant work surface, order-screen/tablet area, numbered food-holding shelves, and separate positions for received orders and orders ready to hand off. Provide a small charging point for couriers outside the operational counter.
Show an affordable-to-investigate construction approach without making up a budget. Keep the form straightforward; invest in clear identity and functioning operations. Do not add kitchens, restaurant sales counters, random dining seating or an autonomous dispensing robot.
Generate a realistic three-quarter exterior view in daylight, showing the attendant, one runner arriving with orders and one courier collecting. Exact Persian title: «تحویل سفارش تپسی‌فود». Exact secondary label: «دریافت پیک». Use the original supplied TapsiFood logo.
Do not inherit the rejected kiosk's 1.50 m by 1.50 m dimensions. The size must follow a workable attendant, shelf and circulation concept, to be verified later. Avoid turning every wall into glazing merely for appearance; keep usable shelf and equipment surfaces.

Design an original TapsiFood engagement and information counter inside a mall food court, based on the concept "A short stop, one useful choice".
Its purpose is to explain services, introduce current campaigns, guide activation of in-person credit shopping where available, and help visitors. This is not a food sales stall, courier dispatch hub or a copy of the foodpanda photo booth.
Use an approachable open counter with two interaction positions:
1. A fast public-facing discovery point: a vertical screen or tactile choice panel with three choices, «پیشنهاد امروز»، «خرید اعتباری»، «راهنمای خدمات».
2. A quieter assisted side position with a tablet, where a staff member can guide activation without exposing the visitor's personal information to the corridor.
Add a distinctive sculptural or illuminated brand frame, replaceable campaign panels, concealed staff storage and a small branded takeaway card/QR surface. The experience ends with a useful action, such as opening a relevant offer or service guide on the visitor's own phone, not just a decorative photo.
Keep visibility through the food court and a clear route past the counter. Show a realistic three-quarter view with one staff member and two visitors. Use original TapsiFood identity, approved Persian typography and a restrained orange/neutral palette. Do not introduce a partner brand in this base concept.
The rejected counter with two food-menu tablets and a burger advertising screen is a diagnostic reference only. Replace that food-sales impression with clear service choices and a dedicated assisted position.
